Our Story

Crafted with
Passion & Fire

Sparman Resturant was born from a simple belief: that great food requires great honesty — honest ingredients, honest technique, and honest warmth. Our chefs tend open wood fires daily, coaxing flavours that no gas burner can replicate.

We source from farms within 150 km of our kitchen, partnering with growers who share our obsession with quality. Every dish on our menu is seasonal, which means it changes — and that's entirely the point.

More Than a Meal

The Experience

🔥

Open Wood Fire Kitchen

Watch our chefs work live over open wood fires — a cooking philosophy that connects every dish to its elemental roots. No shortcuts, no gas, just fire and skill.

🌿

Farm-to-Table Sourcing

Every ingredient has a name and an address. We visit our farm partners personally and let their seasonal harvests guide our ever-changing menu.

🍷

Curated Wine Programme

Our sommelier has handpicked wines from South Africa's finest estates — each pairing designed to elevate the flavours on your plate, not overshadow them.

🎉

Private Dining Room

Host intimate celebrations, corporate dinners, or milestone events in our beautifully appointed private room — bespoke menus available on request.

👨‍🍳

Chef's Table

Sit directly at the pass and experience a curated tasting menu guided by our head chef. Available for groups of 2–6 on selected evenings.

🌙

Ambient Atmosphere

Intimate low lighting, handcrafted oak furnishings, and a curated soundtrack — every element of our space is designed for genuine connection.
